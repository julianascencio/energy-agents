## Evidence and model

Issuer figures are stored separately from synthetic assumptions. The reported 2.8 GW motivates the example scale and the reported GBP 91.20/MWh (2024 prices) is used only as a constant-real energy-value proxy, not a full CfD model. Facility amounts never determine proposed debt or leverage. Actual drawn debt is unknown.

Synthetic inputs: GBP 6.5bn proposed borrowing, 5% real interest, 20 end-year operating payments, minimum DSCR 1.3, GBP 250m annual OPEX, net capacity factors 50% and 40%. The case classifies all calculated results as synthetic. No probabilities or field validation. Model excludes construction finance, tax, fees, reserves, inflation mismatch, CfD detailed settlement and merchant tail. Baseline holds the proposed debt; intervention reduces borrowing to meet the downside threshold. Cost of replacing debt with equity remains unmodelled.

Implementation reuses the finance role and existing permission-enforced orchestrator. `data/debt/skill.json` saves the actual user prompt, mandates, schema, tools, handoffs, output contract and acceptance criteria. Evidence failure blocks finance. No live LLM, trades, contracts, messages or control calls. Provider cost/latency/tokens unknown. Deterministic and scripted workflow outputs must match exactly; no agent-superiority claim.

The downside needs GBP 318.922m less borrowing to meet 1.3 coverage. Break-even net capacity factor is 41.487%. These are scenario outputs, not the issuer's actual economics or savings.
