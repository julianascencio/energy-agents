## Business/customer and economic KPI

Buyer: VPP aggregator or utility programme manager. Offering: paid reserve-envelope
and pre-bid contractual-risk audit, potentially licensed software with appropriate
upstream permission. No customer commitment, willingness-to-pay or product revenue
is asserted. Existing finance role receives a deterministic evidence-gated handoff.

Synthetic 125-home fleet, three cohorts; DC stored energy, AC power and critical
loads explicitly recorded. Three-hour event, 90.25% round-trip efficiency,
4/8/12-hour critical-load reserves. USD 0.60/kWh delivered-energy payment;
USD 0.15/kWh recharge, USD 0.04/kWh wear, USD 0.10/kWh participant compensation,
USD 50/event administration, USD 3/kWh contractual shortfall penalty.

Eight-hour reserve: 480 kW offer, 1,440 kWh delivery, USD 373.06 net/event. Naive
550 kW nameplate offer with the same safe delivery loses USD 256.94/event because
of 210 kWh shortfall. USD 630/event is avoided hypothetical penalty, not realised
income. Break-even delivered-energy payment USD 0.34093/kWh. Abstention retained.
Sensitivity: reserve and shortfall prices. At zero penalty, both offer cash flows
match. No annual extrapolation or stochastic expected value.

## Limits and acceptance

Inputs and results are synthetic/modelled, not field validated. Flags do not verify
consent or telemetry in reality. Reserve feasibility, household load, feeder limits,
export approval, dropout, SOC error, repeated events, recharge availability and
contracts need customer evidence. No inferred capacity payment, retail bill saving,
annual ROI, capex recovery or dispatch authorization. Ineligible cohorts excluded;
initial reserve deficits visible. Fixed tasks enforce evidence before finance.
No trades, contracts, controls, purchases or third-party messages.
